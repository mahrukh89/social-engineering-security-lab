# Disclaimer & Scope

This repository documents a **personal, educational** social engineering exercise conducted entirely within an isolated virtual lab environment.

## Scope

- All systems involved (attacker and target) were personally owned virtual machines
- No real individuals, organizations, or production systems were targeted at any point
- The lab network was isolated (host-only/NAT) with no internet-facing exposure
- No credentials, personal data, or information belonging to real people were collected or stored

## Ethical Use Statement

Social engineering techniques described here are for **educational and awareness purposes only**. They are intended to help understand attacker methodology so that better defenses and training programs can be built.

**Do not** use any concept from this repository against systems, accounts, or people without explicit, documented, written consent. Unauthorized social engineering (including phishing, pretexting, or vishing) against real targets is illegal in most jurisdictions and can carry serious civil and criminal penalties.

## What this repo does *not* contain

- No ready-to-run phishing kits or malicious payloads
- No real victim data or credentials
- No infrastructure for launching attacks outside a lab context

If you have questions about the scope or ethics of this project, feel free to open an issue.
